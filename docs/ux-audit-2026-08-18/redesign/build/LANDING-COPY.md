## Final copy

### 1. Hero
- H1: **Everything you need to apply for a job. Nothing sent for you.**
- Subline: Fit check first. Then a tailored CV, cover letter and emails, grounded in what you've actually done and ready in about two minutes. You review, you send.
- Primary `Start free` · Secondary `See what you get` · Under-CTA: Your first fit check is on us, no account needed.

### 2. Volume band
- H2: **Everyone's applying more. It's working less.**
- Body: AI flooded recruiters with lookalike applications. Filters tightened, good people got buried. Useful Apply is the opposite of a bot. One job, one fit check, one application worth your name.

### 3. Workflow heading
- H2: **One job ad in. A full application out.**

### 4. Step 1: Find
- H3: **A shortlist, not a firehose**
- Body: Every morning, up to 20 fresh roles ranked against your career profile. Matched on skills, seniority and direction, not keyword bingo. Nothing looks right today? Come back tomorrow.

### 5. Step 2: Check the fit
- H3: **Know before you apply.**
- Body: Run the ad against your real CV before you build anything. A clear read on the match and the reasoning behind it. If it's a pass, you lost nothing. If it's a go, you apply like it matters.
- Kicker card: An auto-apply bot needs you to keep applying. Telling you when not to apply isn't in its business model. It's central to ours.

### 6. Step 3: Get the kit
- H3: **The whole kit, built for this job**
- Body: Tailored CV, cover letter, application email and follow-up email. The ad decides what to emphasize, never what's true about you. Facts come from one place: your real CV.
- Second line: Most people never send a follow-up. Yours is already written.

### 7. Step 4: Review and send
- H3: **Change anything by chatting**
- Body: A tighter summary, a different first line, more weight on your last role. Watch it land in the live preview. Six ATS-friendly templates keep the layout clean.
- Bold close: You review everything before it goes anywhere. Then you send it yourself.

### 8. Step 5: Track
- H3: **Every application, remembered**
- Body: Everything you send lands in the tracker. Status, documents and follow-ups in one place, with chat when you want to dig in.

### 9. Founder note (unchanged from live page)
> "I built Useful Apply for my own search. Sixty-plus applications, every one written by the product, five interviews, and the job I have now came from one of them."
> Wasim Jalali, founder

### 10. Pricing
- H2: **Plans you can count** · Intro: Plain units, published monthly prices. You always know what you're buying.
- **Free, $0** `Start free`: 2 full applications a month · Or 8 fit checks while you explore · Top 5 roles a day in your feed
- **Plus, $19.99/mo** (Most popular) `Go Plus`: 25 full applications a month · Fit checks and rewrites from the same allowance · Top 20 roles a day in your feed
- **Max, $79.99/mo** `Go Max`: 100 full applications a month · Fit checks and rewrites from the same allowance · Top 20 roles a day, built for an all-out search
- Footnote: Need more later? Top up with a credit pack for $19.99. Cancel anytime.
- Reassurance: Whichever plan you pick, nothing sends itself. You review, you send.

### 11. Comparison table
- H2: **Why not ChatGPT or a free tool?** · Subline: Same engine class. The difference is the grounding, the memory and the workflow around it.
- Columns: Useful Apply / ChatGPT / Autofill tools. Rows (UA gets a gold check):
  1. Fit check before you spend a minute (✓ / – / –)
  2. Built to stay grounded, nothing added that isn't yours (✓ / – / –)
  3. Remembers your CV and career profile (✓ / – / Varies)
  4. CV, cover letter, application email and follow-up as one kit (✓ / Prompt by prompt / –)
  5. Edit by chatting, live document preview (✓ / – / –)
  6. Daily ranked shortlist and tracker built in (✓ / – / Varies)
- Navy card below, H3: **We prepare, you send.** Useful Apply doesn't submit anything on your behalf. Not today, not ever. Every application waits for your review and your click. That's not a gap we plan to close. It's the whole point.
