## 2. The Thesis

### The market forces a false choice between speed and quality

Every current tool sits in one of three dead quadrants. Karko is the only product in the fourth.

| | **Low quality** | **High quality** |
|---|---|---|
| **High speed** | Auto-apply bots (LazyApply, Sonara, "apply to 200 jobs") | **Karko AI** |
| **Low speed** | ChatGPT prompts, Zety, manual rewriting at midnight | Resume writers, career coaches ($100-500/CV, days) |

- **Fast + low quality:** Auto-apply bots spray generic applications. Candidates get flagged, ghosted, and embarrassed in interviews. ATS vendors are actively hardening against them in 2026.
- **Slow + high quality:** Human resume writers and career coaches. Expensive. Slow. Doesn't scale past one or two applications per week.
- **Slow + low quality:** Generic AI tools and manual rewriting. The default for most job seekers. Hours of work. Output that sounds like ChatGPT.
- **Fast + high quality = Karko.** 90-second application generation with ATS-optimized templates, keyword alignment, and every word traceable to the user's real CV.

### Why now (2026)

1. **Agentic AI moment.** Foundation models are finally reliable enough for full-workflow agents, not just chat. Karko is what happens when the agentic wave hits consumer job search.
2. **Auto-apply backlash.** Spray tools are losing candidate trust. ATS vendors are flagging them. Anti-hallucination becomes the defensible posture.
3. **AI copy fatigue.** Recruiters now spot ChatGPT cover letters in one read. Generic AI is a liability, not an advantage. Human-sounding, traceable output is the new bar.
4. **Job market compression.** Candidates apply to more roles than ever, for longer. They need compression, not more tools.

## 3. What Karko Replaces

Job seekers today stitch together seven to eight separate tools to run a search:

| The seven-tool stack | Karko replaces with |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn / Indeed keyword search | **Smart Job Feed** (personalized, LLM-ranked, up to 25/day) |
| Zety / Canva resume builder | **ATS-optimized templates + inline editor** (6 templates, all ATS-compliant) |
| Jobscan / Resumeworded ATS checker | **Built-in ATS score + keyword injection** |
| ChatGPT "am I a fit?" prompts | **Fit Analysis** (score, strengths, gaps, recommendation) |
| Generic AI cover letter writers | **Cover Letter Generator** (grounded in real CV) |
| Copy-paste email drafting | **Application Email + Follow-up** |
| ChatGPT for screening questions | **Screening Question Answers** (STAR for behavioral, direct for factual) |
| Notion / spreadsheet tracker | **Application Tracker** (auto-saved, status tracking) |

**Compression ratio: 7-8 tools → 1 loop. Roughly 90% time savings.**

The reason Karko can do this: **every module shares one source of truth** (the candidate's real CV). No tool-hopping, no re-entering information, no context loss.

## 4. The Four Pillars

Everything Karko does rests on four product pillars. Every feature, headline, and pitch should trace to one of these.

### Pillar 1 — Anti-hallucination (Trust)
Every CV rewrite, cover letter, email, and screening answer traces back to the candidate's real CV. Karko will not invent experience the user doesn't have. This is the moat. It's why recruiters still trust the applications. It's why candidates don't get embarrassed in interviews.

**Line:** *Never a word you didn't earn.*

### Pillar 2 — Personalization at every layer (Quality)
Every output is personalized three ways: to the user's CV, to the specific role, and to the user's voice. The feed is personal. The fit score is personal. The rewrite is personal. The screening answers are personal.

**Line:** *Tailored to this exact role, from your exact experience.*

### Pillar 3 — Full workflow, one loop (Compression)
The reason Karko saves 90% of the time is the full job application workflow lives in one app, sharing one source of truth. Seven tools, zero tool-switching, zero data re-entry.

**Line:** *One app for your entire job search.*

### Pillar 4 — Human-sounding and ATS-optimized (The unlock)
The output has to survive both the ATS scan AND the recruiter's read. Most tools optimize for one or the other. Karko does both: ATS-compliant templates, keyword injection with a measurable score, and writing that doesn't scream ChatGPT.

**Line:** *Sounds like you. Reads like a real application.*

## 9. Voice & Language Rules

### Do
- **Use second person.** "You," not "users," "candidates," or "job seekers."
- **Use specific numbers.** 25 jobs, 90 seconds, 7 tools, 10 percent. Specifics beat adjectives.
- **Use contractions.** "You're," "it's," "we'll," "don't."
- **Short sentences. One idea per sentence.**
- **Describe the user's current reality before introducing Karko.** Recognition beats persuasion.
- **Use active voice.** "Karko rewrites your CV." Not "Your CV is rewritten by Karko."

### Don't
- **No em dashes.** Ever. They're the AI tell. Replace with commas, colons, periods, or plain hyphens.
- **No hollow fillers.** "Great!", "Absolutely!", "Elevate your career!", "Unlock your potential!" — all cut.
- **No buzzword categories.** Avoid "AI-powered," "next-generation," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "seamless," "agentic system."
- **No "leverage," "utilize," "empower."** Say "use" or cut the sentence.
- **No third-person hooks.** "Most people..." is weak. "You..." is strong.
- **No claims you can't back.** Don't fabricate conversion rates or time saves. Use the founder's real numbers.

### Words that earn their keep
Grounded. Traceable. Tailored. Real. Full workflow. Loop. Compressed. Matched. Honest.

### Words to avoid
Leverage. Empower. Utilize. Seamless. Revolutionary. Next-gen. AI-powered. Unlock. Elevate. Supercharge. Game-changer. Robust. Cutting-edge. Agentic system. Synergy. Streamline.

## 9.5. Content Framing by Format

Different formats earn attention differently. The category frame ("seven tools, one app") works in long content. It fails in short content, where you have 3 seconds to hook before the viewer scrolls. This section locks how to frame Karko across every content format.

### The core rule

> **Long content sells the category. Short content sells one pain, then stamps the category.**

You cannot sell "all-in-one" in 15 seconds. You can sell one pain and reveal the product is bigger than that one feature. That is the short-content move.

### Framing by format

| Format | Length | Hook strategy | End-stamp |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts** | 15-30s | ONE feature + ONE pain. Open with a vivid moment ("8 essay questions in the portal"), not a feature name. | "Part of Karko. Your entire job search, in one app." |
| **Twitter/X video** | 30-60s | Stack TWO pains. Bridge to Karko. | Tagline at end, or "Karko. One app for all of it." |
| **LinkedIn video** | 45-90s | Full compression story ("the seven tools you juggle today..."). Category frame works here. | Tagline A or tagline B depending on audience. |
| **YouTube long-form, demo, podcast, walkthrough** | 60s-10min+ | Full category story. All features. "Seven tools → one app" lands because you have runway. | Both taglines. Demo script v4 is the canonical example. |
| **Homepage hero video** | 85-90s | Full story. YC-standard demo length. | Tagline A on the end-card. |
| **Investor pitch video / deck** | 2-3 min | Lead with category ("Karko is the AI job application agent"). Name the seven tools. Name the two moats. Founder dogfood stat. | Tagline B on the cover slide. |

### Short-form content playbook (the 10 videos to build first)

Build a library of 10 short videos. Each one picks ONE feature and ONE pain. All end with the same stamp. Consistency is the lever.

1. **Screening questions** — Hook: "You found the job. Then you hit 8 essay questions." Reveal: Karko drafts them from your real CV in 2 minutes.
2. **Smart Job Feed** — Hook: "You're spending 10 hours a week on LinkedIn. Stop." Reveal: 25 hand-picked roles delivered every morning.
3. **Fit analysis** — Hook: "Stop wasting an hour on jobs you'll never get." Reveal: Half a credit shows match score + gaps before you write.
4. **Anti-hallucination** — Hook: "Your AI cover letter is lying about your experience." Reveal: Karko will not invent experience you do not have.
5. **Cover letter speed** — Hook: "You're writing cover letters at midnight." Reveal: 90 seconds, tailored to the role.
6. **Follow-up email** — Hook: "Most candidates never follow up." Reveal: Karko pre-drafts the follow-up for 7 to 10 days after you apply.
7. **Tracker** — Hook: "Which company was that one again?" Reveal: Every application saves automatically with match score and status.
8. **ATS templates** — Hook: "76% of resumes get rejected by robots." Reveal: Every Karko template is ATS-safe by default.
9. **Founder story** — Hook: "I ran out of money in Germany building a startup." Reveal: 10% interview rate using Karko vs 2-3% industry baseline.
10. **Real output** — Screen-record a 90-second generation end to end. No voice, just text overlays and the product. Pure proof.

**Every one of the 10 ends with:** a 2-second end card showing the Karko logo + the tagline **"Your entire job search, in one app."**

### Long-form content playbook

For long-form (YouTube walkthroughs, podcasts, demo videos, webinars), follow the v4 demo/walkthrough scripts (`Karko_AI_Video_Scripts_v4.html` on the founder's desktop). Those are the canonical templates.

Key structural move for long content:
1. Open with a pain-stab hook (same as short form).
2. **Bridge beat:** "Karko replaces the seven tools you use to apply for a job. One app, one loop." (This is the sentence you cannot fit into 15 seconds.)
3. Walk through the loop: Feed → Upload → Fit → Full Application → Screening → Tracker.
4. Close with a concrete first action ("Pick one role, run it through") + tagline.

### Platform-specific taglines at the end

Always end with the right tagline for the audience:

| Audience | Tagline to use |
|---|---|
| Job seekers (consumer platforms) | Tagline A: "Your entire job search, in one app." |
| Investors, press, career pages, recruiter-facing content | Tagline B: "The AI job application agent." |
| Mixed (LinkedIn videos that reach both) | Both, in the two-line format |

### The one thing that multiplies this strategy

**Consistency across platforms.** A job seeker who sees one 15-second screening video on TikTok, one smart-feed post on LinkedIn, and lands on a homepage that says the same tagline all three places will remember the brand. Fragmentation kills positioning.

Pick the taglines. Pick the end-card. Ship every piece of content with both. Do not let any single video or ad skip the end-card stamp.

## 12. The Test (run before shipping any new copy)

Any new piece of copy should pass three questions before it ships:

1. **Does it describe the user's current reality before it describes Karko?** If not, rewrite.
2. **Could this sentence appear in a Zety or LazyApply ad?** If yes, it's too generic. Rewrite until it can only describe Karko.
3. **Does a job seeker reading this recognize their own Sunday night?** If not, it's not specific enough.

If all three pass, ship it.

## 13. What Karko is NOT (for internal clarity and FAQ framing)

These are the five categories people might file Karko under that are all wrong. Every time a competitor ships a feature and you feel pressure to add something, re-read this section before committing engineering time.

- **Not a resume builder.** Builders assume you start with a blank page. Karko starts with your real CV and tailors it per role.
- **Not an auto-apply bot.** Bots optimize for volume. Karko optimizes for quality and traceability. The user stays in control. Auto-apply bots are dying in 2026 because ATS vendors flag them and candidates get burned in interviews.
- **Not a generic AI writer.** Generic AI produces generic output. Karko produces output grounded in the specific user and specific role.
- **Not a career coach.** Coaches help with strategy and long-term positioning. Karko handles the execution loop once the user knows what they want.
- **Not ChatGPT with a job-search UI.** Karko is a workflow product. The AI is the engine, not the interface.

### Terminology note (Primary CV vs Master CV)

These are **two different concepts** in the product. Do not conflate them.

- **Primary CV** = the 1-2 page CV the user uploads (or pastes) to send to employers. This is the source of truth every generated output traces back to. It is the "upload your CV" moment in every flow. On the features page, this is the first card.
- **Master CV** = an optional, 5-10 page comprehensive inventory the user builds via the guide at `/master-cv`. It is a *reference* document for users whose real experience does not fit into a 1-2 page CV. Most users will not build one. The guide lives in the footer under Resources, not in the Product column.

If copy anywhere treats them as the same thing, that is a bug. Primary CV is the core product concept. Master CV is an optional power-user reference tool.
