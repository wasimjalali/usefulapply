# Karko AI — Product Features

> Every feature exists for one reason: to save job seekers real time and get them better results. No fluff, no buzzwords — just what the product does and why it matters.

---

## Core Application Generator

### Smart Job Feed
**What it does:** Builds a daily list of roles based on the user's target titles, locations, market preferences, and CV. Jobs are ranked by fit so the user starts with opportunities that are more likely to be worth applying to.

**Value for job seekers:** Instead of opening five job boards every morning, users get a curated feed inside Karko. They can save roles, dismiss poor matches, open employer sites, or send a strong role directly into the Application Kit.

**Availability:** Plus includes up to 15 curated jobs per day. Max includes up to 25 curated jobs per day.

---

### AI-Tailored CV Optimization
**What it does:** Takes your existing CV and the job description, then rewrites your CV to match what the employer is looking for — highlighting relevant experience, adding missing keywords, and restructuring sections for maximum impact.

**Value for job seekers:** You stop sending the same generic CV to every job. Each application gets a CV that reads like you wrote it specifically for that role, which dramatically increases your callback rate. The AI only uses facts from your actual CV — it never invents experience you don't have.

**Cost:** Included in the 2-credit full application.

---

### Cover Letter Generation
**What it does:** Generates a cover letter that connects your specific experience to what the company needs. References the company's culture, recent news, and values when available. Matches the language of the job posting (English, German, etc.).

**Value for job seekers:** Writing a good cover letter from scratch takes 30-45 minutes. This gives you a solid first draft in seconds that you can review and tweak. It actually references your real experience — not generic templates.

**Cost:** Included in the 2-credit full application. Regeneration: 0.5 credits.

---

### Application Email
**What it does:** Writes a professional email to submit your application, with appropriate subject line, greeting, and body text. Matches the formality level and language of the job posting.

**Value for job seekers:** No more staring at a blank email wondering what to write. The email is ready to copy-paste into your email client, with the right tone for the market (formal for German applications, direct for US/UK).

**Cost:** Included in the 2-credit full application. Regeneration: 0.5 credits.

---

### Follow-Up Email
**What it does:** Generates a polite follow-up email to send if you haven't heard back, with a recommended delay (usually 5-7 business days). References the specific role and adds a brief value reminder.

**Value for job seekers:** Most candidates never follow up, and those who do often sound desperate or pushy. This gives you a professional follow-up that keeps you top of mind without being annoying.

**Cost:** Included in the 2-credit full application. Regeneration: 0.5 credits.

---

## Fit Analysis (Analyze First Mode)

### Job Fit Score
**What it does:** Before you spend credits on a full application, the AI analyzes the job description against your CV and gives you an honest match score with a detailed breakdown — matching skills, missing skills, and suggestions.

**Value for job seekers:** You stop wasting time applying to jobs you're not qualified for. If the fit score is low, you know to move on. If it's high, you apply with confidence. Either way, you save time.

**Cost:** 0.5 credits for the analysis. If you then apply, the full application costs 2 credits (2.5 total for the analyze-first flow).

---

### Skill Gap Analysis
**What it does:** Shows you exactly which skills and qualifications you have that match the job, which ones you're missing, and actionable suggestions for how to position yourself despite the gaps.

**Value for job seekers:** You learn what employers in your target roles actually want. Over multiple analyses, you see patterns — if every job asks for a skill you lack, that's your signal to learn it. It turns job searching into career development.

## CV Templates

### 6 ATS-Optimized Templates
**What it does:** Your optimized CV is rendered into one of six professional templates, all designed to pass Applicant Tracking Systems. Templates use system fonts (Arial + Georgia) that every ATS can read.

| Template | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| **Premium ATS** | Default for most applications |
| **Modern Professional** | Tech and startup roles |
| **Executive** | Senior and leadership positions |
| **German ATS** | German market (two-column with photo) |
| **German Classic** | German market (single-column traditional) |
| **Classic Green** | All industries, clean design |

**Value for job seekers:** You don't need to worry about formatting. Every template is tested to parse correctly in major ATS systems (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SAP SuccessFactors). Your CV looks professional to humans and readable to machines.

---

### Inline CV Editor
**What it does:** After the AI generates your optimized CV, you can click into any section and edit text directly. Change wording, add details, remove bullets — all within the template layout.

**Value for job seekers:** You keep full control. The AI gives you a strong starting point, but you can fine-tune every line before downloading. No need to export, edit in Word, and re-import.

---

### PDF Download
**What it does:** One-click download of your finalized CV as a clean, properly formatted PDF.

**Value for job seekers:** The PDF is ready to attach and send. No conversion issues, no broken formatting.

## Document Management

### Master CV
**What it does:** Upload your complete career history as a "Master CV" — every role, skill, certification, and project you've ever had. The AI uses this as its source of truth when tailoring CVs for specific jobs.

**Value for job seekers:** You upload once and the AI pulls the right experience for each application. Your five-year-old project management role gets highlighted for PM jobs, while your recent data engineering work gets emphasized for tech roles — all from the same master document.

---

### CV Parsing
**What it does:** Upload a PDF or document and the system extracts your information into structured sections — personal details, experience, education, skills, languages, certifications, and projects.

**Value for job seekers:** You don't have to manually type anything. Upload your existing CV and the system understands it. Works with most CV formats, including those with complex layouts, tables, and multi-column designs.

**Technical note:** Uses a multi-layer parsing pipeline — PDF.js extraction first, binary fallback for edge cases, and AI-powered document understanding for PDFs with broken fonts or unusual encoding.

---

### Certifications Storage
**What it does:** Upload and store your professional certifications alongside your CV documents. These are included in your profile for the AI to reference.

**Value for job seekers:** Certifications are often the difference between getting past the first filter and being rejected. Having them stored means the AI always includes relevant ones in your tailored CV.

## AI Chat Assistant

### Contextual Chat
**What it does:** A chat interface where you can ask questions about your application, the job, or get advice. The AI has full context of your CV, the job description, and everything it generated.

**Value for job seekers:** You can ask things like "What should I emphasize in the interview?" or "How do I explain this career gap?" and get answers that are specific to your actual situation and the specific job — not generic career advice.

---

### AI Screening Question Answers (Max Plan)
**What it does:** After your application is generated, paste the screening questions from the job portal and the AI drafts personalized answers. Each answer is formatted based on the question type — short factual answers for yes/no questions, structured STAR-method responses for behavioral questions, and step-by-step approaches for situational questions. Each answer has its own copy button.

**Value for job seekers:** Screening questions are the most tedious part of online applications. Some portals have 5-10 questions, and answering them well takes 20-30 minutes per application. The AI uses your CV and the job context to draft answers that are truthful, specific to your experience, and properly sized for each question type. You copy, review, paste, and move on.

**Cost:** No additional credits — included with Max plan subscription. Limited to 2 uses per application session.

---

## Application Tracking

### Application Tracker
**What it does:** Every application you generate is saved with the job title, company, match score, and date. You can see all your applications in one dashboard with status tracking (applied, interview, rejected, accepted, follow-up).

**Value for job seekers:** When you're applying to dozens of jobs, you lose track. The tracker shows you where you stand with every application — which ones need follow-up, which ones moved to interview, and your overall hit rate.

---

### Session History
**What it does:** Every application session (job description, generated materials, chat history) is saved and can be loaded back. You can revisit any past application.

**Value for job seekers:** If a recruiter calls about a job you applied to three weeks ago, you can pull up the exact CV and cover letter you sent them in seconds. No digging through email or folders.

---

## Company Intelligence

### Automated Company Research
**What it does:** When generating your application, the AI researches the company — extracting culture, values, tech stack, and recent news from the job posting and available information. This context is woven into your cover letter and email.

**Value for job seekers:** Your cover letter doesn't just say "I'm interested in your company." It references specific things about the company that show you've done your homework. Hiring managers notice when candidates actually know something about the company.

---

## Anti-Hallucination Guardrails

### Fact-Grounded Generation
**What it does:** Every claim in your optimized CV, cover letter, and email must trace back to your actual CV or Master CV. The job description is used for keyword matching and context only — never as a source of facts about you.

**Value for job seekers:** The AI will never claim you have skills or experience you don't actually have. If the job asks for Python and you've never used Python, the AI won't add it to your CV. This protects you from embarrassment in interviews and maintains your professional integrity.

---

## Multi-Language Support

### Automatic Language Detection
**What it does:** The AI detects the language of the job description and generates all materials (CV, cover letter, email, screening answers) in that same language. Supports English, German, and other European languages.

**Value for job seekers:** If you're applying to a German-language job posting, everything comes out in German — proper grammar, formal/informal register, and culturally appropriate formatting (photo on CV, date format, etc.). No manual translation needed.

## Technical Foundation

- **AI Model:** Claude Sonnet 4.6 by Anthropic — all plans, no downgraded models for free users
- **ATS Compliance:** System fonts (Arial + Georgia), clean PDF structure, no images-as-text, no fancy layouts that break parsers
- **Security:** No secrets in client code, authenticated API routes, rate limiting, Stripe webhook verification
- **Data Privacy:** Your CV data is stored in your account only. No training on user data.
- **Deployment:** Vercel (production), Supabase (database + auth + storage), Stripe (payments)
