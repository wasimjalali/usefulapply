# CV/Resume Template Design Research Request

## Context
I'm developing a job application assistant that generates optimized CVs/resumes for users. The application currently has 6 CV templates (3 for US/Canada, 3 for EU/Germany) but needs expert guidance to ensure they follow industry best practices and standards that maximize success with both ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) and human recruiters.

## Research Questions

### 1. Industry Standards & Best Practices (2024-2025)

**For US/Canada Market:**
- What are the current ATS optimization best practices? (keywords, formatting, section order)
- Should resumes be strictly 1 page or is 2 pages acceptable for experienced professionals?
- What font families are most ATS-friendly and professional? (serif vs sans-serif debate)
- What font sizes are optimal? (name, headings, body text, contact info)
- What are the recommended margins? (top, bottom, left, right)
- Should contact info include LinkedIn/GitHub? What about physical address?
- Are icons (email, phone, location) ATS-safe or should they be avoided?
- What color schemes are considered professional but distinctive?
- Should skills be listed as categories or flat lists?
- What's the optimal section order for ATS parsing?

**For EU/Germany Market:**
- Is a professional photo mandatory or optional in 2024? (considering anti-discrimination laws)
- What are the photo specifications? (size, background, dress code, quality)
- Should CVs include date of birth and nationality? (GDPR considerations)
- What's the standard CV length in EU? (2 pages? more?)
- Are Europass-style formats still relevant or outdated?
- What are German-specific requirements (Lebenslauf standards)?
- Should language proficiency use CEFR levels (A1-C2) or descriptive terms?
- What's the expected format for education dates and work experience dates?
- Are two-column/sidebar layouts acceptable or should CVs be single-column?
