Senior/Software Engineer II, Backend
New York, NY; Pittsburgh, PA

Our mission at Duolingo is to develop the best education in the world and make it universally available. It’s a big mission, and that’s where you come in!

We’re looking for backend engineers who have experience building and operating reliable, scalable systems. Ads:

Duolingo's Monetization pillar builds the systems that fund our mission - from subscription packaging and advertising to ML-powered personalization. As a Backend Engineer on the Ads team, you'll work on a product engineering team responsible for growing and scaling Duolingo's ads business: high-quality ads that delight learners, fit seamlessly into the product experience, and meaningfully contribute to our bottom line. AI Platform:

As a member of the AI Tooling and Ecosystem Team, you will be responsible for developing and maintaining tools and services that power Duolingo's use of AI in the product.
