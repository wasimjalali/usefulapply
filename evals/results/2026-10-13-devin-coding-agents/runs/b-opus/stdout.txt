**Choices the spec didn't settle:**
- If a title starts with a delimiter, like "(Senior) Engineer", the primary title would be empty, so it keeps the full title.
- If not even one word fits in step c, the subject falls back to the bare prefix.
- Length counts UTF-16 units, which is never fewer than the visible characters, so a subject can't go over 60.
- Trailing punctuation removed after a cut is `. , ; : ! ? & / | (` and the three dashes. `#` and `+` are kept so titles like "C#" and "C++" survive.
